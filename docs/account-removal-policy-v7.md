# Account removal policy

Penny treats an account as actively in use only when it is referenced by a transaction, income record or bank-balance snapshot in a month that is not marked complete.

- Accounts with no references can be removed.
- Accounts referenced only by completed historical months can be removed from the active account list and future choices.
- Accounts still referenced by any open/incomplete month remain protected until those records are reassigned or the month is completed.
- Removing an historical-only account does not delete or rewrite completed-month records. Stored account labels and bank-balance evidence remain intact.
- Account-list changes continue to be recorded in Change History.
