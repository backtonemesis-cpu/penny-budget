# Uniform fresh-month empty states

- Income now says `No income recorded.` when the selected month has no income.
- Expenses now says `No expenses recorded.` when the selected month has no expenses.
- Savings now says `No savings recorded.` when the selected month has no savings snapshot.
- A genuinely empty Savings month no longer shows a zero snapshot total or the Savings Goal panel, keeping the Income, Expenses and Savings fresh-month views visually consistent.
