# Monthly Income Detail

- Tapping the Overview Income card now opens a dedicated Income Detail view for the selected month.
- Every income record remains visible after entry with source/type, amount or TBC, exact date status, recipient, receiving account and received/expected status.
- Income Detail supports add, edit, status changes and delete when the month is editable.
- The Income Detail total reconciles to the same monthly income total shown on Overview.
- Completed months remain viewable even when editing is locked.
