# Negative savings overview v91

- Negative savings-account balances now display in red instead of green.
- Savings Snapshot/Closing Savings totals also switch to red when the recorded total is below zero.
- Current savings totals now sum signed balances instead of silently treating negative savings as zero.
- Overview Total savings continues to mean end-of-month total: savings snapshot plus the selected month's income-minus-expenses result.
- The four-card Overview keeps its Total savings colour synchronised when the value changes from positive to negative or back again.
- Transfer Plan current bank balances also use the signed stored balance consistently in calculation logic.
- Income, expenses, savings goals and monthly contributions remain non-negative.
