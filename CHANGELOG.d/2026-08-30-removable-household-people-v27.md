# Removable household people v27

- Household people can now be removed from the current Penny master list once they no longer own any active account.
- Historical transactions and income do not permanently lock a person in Settings; their saved person IDs/labels remain preserved for audit traceability.
- Active account ownership still blocks removal until the account is reassigned or removed.
- Clear Month scope, calculations, historical records, audit storage, categories, savings and reconciliation logic are unchanged.
