# 2026-08-30 — Start-of-month bank balances default to zero

- Current bank balance in the Start-of-Month Transfer Plan now defaults to £0.00 when no balance has been entered.
- Transfer needed is calculated immediately from planned costs minus the default £0.00 balance.
- The balance remains fully editable; clearing it returns the effective balance to £0.00 rather than TBC.
- Ambiguous/shared accounts that must be separated still remain TBC until resolved.
