# v75 — Desktop month picker

- Replaces the generic desktop browser month popup with a Penny-designed month/year picker.
- Keeps the iPhone/native mobile month wheel unchanged.
- Desktop picker shows the selected year with previous/next year controls and a 12-month grid.
- Adds a `This month` shortcut and closes on month selection, outside click, or Escape.
- Preserves the existing `YYYY-MM` month value, month-selection behaviour, finance calculations, storage, header height, and bottom navigation.
- Adds a dedicated desktop month picker regression test.
