- Restores the v75 Penny-designed desktop month/year picker as the desktop selection path.
- Keeps the working native iPhone/mobile month picker unchanged.
- Limits the v78 native `showPicker()` activation helper to mobile so it cannot override desktop behaviour.
- Preserves the v75 desktop controls: previous/next year, 12-month grid, selected month styling, outside-click/Escape close, and `This month` shortcut.
- No finance calculations, saved data, storage, account logic, or header layout are changed.
- Extends regression coverage to ensure desktop does not invoke the native picker.
